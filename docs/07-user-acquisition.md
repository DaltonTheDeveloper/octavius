# Free user acquisition — getting your first players

You can reach the front page with **zero ad spend**. The biggest 2025 hits were
powered by short-form video, memes, and cross-game stunts — not ad budgets.
Because the algorithm ignores ad-acquired users (`06`), free organic growth is
also the *only* growth that lifts your ranking.

## 1. Short-form video is the dominant free channel

Roblox is the most-discussed game on TikTok (~**1 in 5** gaming videos mention
it), and Roblox content has crossed **1 trillion** YouTube views. Roblox even
launched **"Roblox Moments"**, an in-app TikTok-style feed that one-tap teleports
viewers into the featured experience.
[Tubefilter](https://www.tubefilter.com/2025/09/09/roblox-is-entering-its-tiktok-era/)

Case studies: **Steal a Brainrot** rode a pre-existing TikTok meme ("Italian
brainrot") so its characters were recognizable before launch, then went viral on
**emotional reaction clips** (kids crying when their Brainrot is stolen).
**Grow a Garden** + Steal a Brainrot's cross-game **"admin war" event** drove the
platform's 47.4M-CCU record.

Clipping best practices (community/vendor, directional): post **1–2×/day,
≥5×/week**; **15–30s** clips; **hook in the first 2 seconds**; 3–5 hashtags
(#roblox #robloxfyp #fyp); best window ~6–9 PM EST, Thu–Sun. Follower count
doesn't gate reach — viral Roblox clips routinely come from <1k-follower accounts.
[BLOXG](https://bloxg.com/guides/roblox-tiktok-marketing)

## 2. In-game viral loops (build these in)

- **Friend Referral System** (official, ~April 2025): players share referral
  links; both sides get rewards. Read `ReferredByPlayerId` from
  `Player:GetJoinData()`; configure the reward banner in Creator Hub → Engagement
  → Referral Rewards.
  [referral](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/referral-system)
- **Play-with-friends rewards**: co-play sessions run ~1.9× longer and co-play is
  a ranking signal — reward inviting/joining friends directly.
- **Reward social actions** (in-game currency for inviting, joining the group/
  Discord, photo/share moments). *Do not* lead your title/thumbnail with
  giveaways — that's penalized (`06`).

## 3. Community

Top games run massive Discords (Grow a Garden's has ~5.26M members); Discord
offers official integrations for top experiences. Roblox tracks **Community
Growth** (joining your Group/Discord) as a metric. Practical advice from devs:
**master one platform first** with daily personal engagement (the creator plays
with players and invites them in), run weekly creation contests with shoutouts,
and let the community vote on features.
[community](https://medium.com/roblox-developer/from-the-devs-growing-and-leveraging-a-passionate-community-on-roblox-by-ben-est-8aad3d53a1b)

## 4. Store-listing SEO

Keyword weight is **title > description > tags**; semantic search now handles
natural-language queries.
[SEO](https://rolearn.dev/guidance/roblox-game-seo-title-description-tags)

- **Title** ≤50 chars (30–50 optimal); all-caps doesn't help; front-load
  keywords; keep it unique (copycat titles are penalized).
- **Description**: the first ~100 chars carry the most weight and show in
  previews — front-load keywords there.
- **Tags**: fill all slots, but never misleading (triggers moderation).

## 5. Update cadence as acquisition

Frequent updates feed "Recently Updated" sorts and bring players back inside the
D1/D2-7/D8-28 windows (`08`). Home-recommendation traffic peaks **Saturdays** —
time launches/updates accordingly.

## 6. UGC items

Uploading a UGC item costs ~750 Robux (~$7.50) — a cheap way to put wearable,
game-branded items into circulation.

## Paid ads — context only

Roblox **Ads Manager** offers Sponsored Experiences, Search Ads, and Image Ads
(CPM/CPP/CPC, second-price auction, $300/day cap). It buys a spike and gets you
into the candidate pool, but **ad-acquired users don't lift ranking** — so for a
$0 budget, everything above matters more. [ads](https://www.guptamedia.com/insights/roblox-advertising)
